# Classroom quick start — after publishing

1. Teacher opens the deployed Cloudflare `workers.dev` game website.
2. Teacher clicks **Host a Cloudflare game room** and **Create live classroom room**.
3. Teacher clicks **Copy student link** and posts it in Canvas.
4. One Chromebook per team opens that link, chooses a European country,
   and requests a seat. No code entry, accounts, or installs for students.
5. Teacher approves and assigns exactly seven different European powers.
6. Teacher starts and advances the turn order and projects public map view.
7. On their turns, students click regions, review odds, and commit moves;
   other teams can prepare offers. Diplomatic offers stay private until
   resolved. The teacher can see all information.
8. At the end, compare the alternate-history map to a reliable 1914
   historical atlas map. Ethiopian and Liberian independence are part of
   the historical debrief.

**Before first use:** run a seven-Chromebook pilot and test the exact
Canvas-posted room link. The published site must be allowed by school
filtering. Do not enter student personal information.

**Publishing instructions:** see START_HERE_NO_INSTALL.txt.
